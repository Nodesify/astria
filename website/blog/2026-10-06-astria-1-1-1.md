**1.1.1** is a maintenance release with no user-facing behavior changes. It updates the native-module packaging toolchain to NAPI CLI v3, adopts TypeScript 7.0.2 for the CLI and viewer, and refreshes dependencies and CI tooling.

<!-- truncate -->

The musl probe now checks the known compiler condition explicitly. See the [1.1.1 changelog](https://github.com/Nodesify/astria/blob/v1.1.1/CHANGELOG.md) for the complete maintenance summary.
